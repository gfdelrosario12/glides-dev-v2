## Purpose

Defines the presentation of social links with scannable QR codes for easy mobile access.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Social media links include QR codes
The system SHALL display the user's social media profiles in a dedicated section, providing both clickable links and scannable QR codes for each profile.

#### Scenario: Viewing social links
- **WHEN** a visitor views the social section
- **THEN** they can scan a QR code to open the corresponding social media profile on their mobile device
