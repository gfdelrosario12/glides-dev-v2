## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: The expertise section presents operational focus areas and circular language levels
The section SHALL be presented as "Expertise Summary". It SHALL display operational infrastructure domains alongside circular proficiency gauges (0–100) for tracked programming languages, using color-coded progress rings to visually communicate technical depth.

#### Scenario: Inspecting language proficiency indicators
- **WHEN** a visitor views the language section within the Expertise Summary
- **THEN** each language is rendered with a circular progress gauge indicating its level from 0 to 100 with corresponding telemetry coloring
